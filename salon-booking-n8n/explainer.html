n8n + Square + Telnyx

Salon Booking Autopilot

Two n8n workflows. One answers client texts and books appointments. The other sends confirmations, reminders and staff alerts. Square keeps the calendar and the saved cards.

Who does what

SquareThe calendar, each stylist's own service times, saved cards, and no-show fees.
TelnyxThe salon's texting number. Sends and receives every text.
n8nThe brain in the middle. Runs the two workflows below, 24/7.
Workflow A

The AI that answers texts

This is how it looks inside n8n. Each box is one step, and the arrows show the order. The purple dashed lines connect the AI to its brain, its memory and its Square tools.

On a phone, swipe the drawing sideways to see all of it.

  1. Text comes inA client texts the salon number. Telnyx passes the message to n8n.
  2. SettingsYour salon details: name, phones, stylists, services and how long each stylist takes.
  3. Find client in SquareLooks up the phone number to see if this person is already a client.
  4. Known client?Yes: go check for a saved card. No: they're new.
  5. Check saved cardDoes Square already have a card on file for them?
  6. Prepare the AIWrites the AI's instructions: today's date, the services, the rules, and whether this client can book by text.
  7. AI assistantClaude reads the text and replies like a receptionist. It remembers the conversation.
  8. Square toolsThe AI's four buttons: check open times, book, list my bookings, cancel. It only offers times Square says are free.
  9. Tidy the replyCleans up the answer and notices if a person needs to step in.
  10. Text the clientSends the AI's answer back through Telnyx.
  11. Needs a person?If yes, texts the manager and pauses the AI for that client for 12 hours.

Workflow B

Confirmations, reminders and alerts

This one has two starting points: Square telling us something changed, and a clock that checks every hour. Both paths meet in the middle and end by sending texts.

  1. Square booking changedSquare tells n8n right away when a booking is made, cancelled or marked no-show.
  2. Every hourA clock that checks the calendar once an hour for reminders.
  3. SettingsThe same salon details as the other workflow.
  4. Check it's from SquareSecurity check, then sorts it: new booking, cancelled, late cancel or no-show.
  5. Get upcoming bookingsPulls the next two days of appointments from Square.
  6. Who needs a reminder?Finds visits 2 days away, 2 hours away, or that just finished.
  7. Get client from SquareGets the client's name and phone number.
  8. Write the textsWrites the right message for the right person: client, stylist or manager.
  9. Send textSends each message through Telnyx.

Who gets which text

ClientBooking confirmation, reminder 2 days before, reminder 2 hours before, thank-you with a review link.
StylistNew booking heads-up, and a note when a time opens up from a cancellation.
ManagerLate cancels and no-shows (charge the fee in Square), and any client who asked for a person.
