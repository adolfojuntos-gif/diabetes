The main idea

Same service, each stylist's own time

Square gives every service one length. So we make one version of the service for each speed. The client sees the same name and price either way. The calendar just blocks the right amount of time for the stylist they pick.

StylistLong haircut takesFits in an 8-hour day
Maria (fast)45 min + 10 min clean-upabout 8–9 long cuts
Ana90 min + 10 min clean-upabout 4–5 long cuts

Mixed with shorter services, that is how a fast stylist reaches 14–15 clients a day and another stays at 9–10, without anyone being asked to speed up. The numbers are examples. Each stylist sets their own times.

The 5 parts

What each automation does

Part 1 · Text assistant

The AI that books appointments

  • Reads the client's text
  • Checks the Square calendar for open times
  • Asks for a clear YES before booking
  • Can also cancel or move a booking
  • Passes the chat to a person if it gets stuck
Part 2 · Save-card page

The safe page for the card

  • Card goes into Square's own secure box
  • The salon never sees the full card number
  • Client ticks "I agree to the policy"
  • The date and time they agreed is saved as proof
Part 3 · Reminders

Texts that go out by themselves

  • 2 days before: "Free to change until…"
  • 2 hours before: "See you soon!"
  • After the visit: "Leave us a review?"
Part 4 · Calendar watcher

Knows when bookings change

  • New booking: texts the stylist
  • Cancelled in time: texts the stylist, no fee
  • Late cancel or no-show: texts the manager
Part 5 · Charge or waive

The manager decides about every fee

  • The manager taps the link and sees who, when and how much
  • Two buttons: Charge or Waive (with a reason, like "family emergency")
  • Charge: Square charges the saved card, and the client gets a polite receipt text
  • Every decision goes into a Google Sheet, so there's a clear record
Start to finish

One appointment, step by step

  1. Client texts the salon"Anything tomorrow at 2?"
  2. AI checks the calendarOnly real open times, using that stylist's own service length.
  3. Client picks a timeThe AI repeats the details and waits for YES.
  4. Card is savedFirst-time clients get the secure link. Returning clients already have one.
  5. Booking is made in SquareIt shows up on the salon calendar like any other booking.
  6. Reminders go out2 days before, 2 hours before, and a thank-you after.
  7. If they don't showThe stylist taps No-show in Square. The manager chooses Charge or Waive.
Built to be safe

Why clients and staff can trust it

No card numbers by text

Cards only go into Square's secure page. The AI is told never to ask for them.

No surprise charges

Money only moves when the manager presses Charge. Links can't be faked.

No rushing stylists

Each stylist's times are their own. The system doesn't rank or compare anyone.

What you need

The pieces

The automation engine. It runs the 5 parts behind the scenes.

The salon's texting number. It needs to be registered for business texting in the US.
