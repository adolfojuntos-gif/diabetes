How to use

Your first injection takes about 60 seconds.

General steps for a once-weekly pen. Always follow the instructions on your prescription and your provider's dosing plan.

01

Wash & prep

Wash hands. Check the label, expiry and that the liquid is clear. Attach a new needle.

02

Pick a site

Belly (2 in. from the navel), front of thigh, or back of upper arm. Rotate every week.

03

Clean the skin

Wipe with an alcohol swab and let it air-dry for a few seconds.

04

Set your dose

Turn the dial to the dose your provider prescribed. Never adjust it yourself.

05

Inject

Press straight in at 90°, hold the button, and count slowly to 6 before lifting.

06

Dispose & store

Needle into a sharps container. Pen back in the fridge (36–46°F). Never freeze.

FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is this the same as Ozempic® or Wegovy®?

Ozempic and Wegovy are brand names for semaglutide; Mounjaro and Zepbound are brand names for tirzepatide. Your provider prescribes the medication that's appropriate and legally available, and you'll see exactly which product is dispensed before you pay.

Who qualifies for GLP-1 weight-loss medication?

Generally adults with a BMI of 30+, or 27+ with a weight-related condition such as high blood pressure, prediabetes or high cholesterol. A licensed provider makes the final decision based on your full history.

How fast will I see results?

Many people notice less appetite in the first week or two. Weight loss is gradual as the dose rises over the first few months, and most of the loss happens between months 4 and 12.

What are the side effects?

Mostly digestive (nausea, diarrhoea, constipation), most common while the dose is increasing, and usually easing over time. Your care team can adjust your plan. Serious but rare risks include pancreatitis and gallbladder problems; your provider reviews these with you.

Do skin and collagen products need a prescription?

No. Serums are cosmetics and collagen is a dietary supplement. They ship as soon as you order.

Do you sell BPC-157 or TB-500?

No. They aren't approved for human use, the FDA restricts compounding them, and both are banned in tested sport (WADA). Our athlete line is built on ingredients that are legal, tested and safe for competition.

Yes, anytime from your account. No contracts, no cancellation fees.
